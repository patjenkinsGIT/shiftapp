EcoPay

The AI that pays you back.

SHIFT returns a share of its gross AI-credit revenue to members. It pays only on new credit purchases, and it distributes that share through several streams.

Think airline loyalty, but paid in cash instead of miles, and triggered by other people's AI use as well as your own.

The streams

Five ways it pays

01
ClubPay

Pooled payouts to club members from ecosystem credit purchases. The higher your membership club, the more pools you draw from.

02
CoachPay

A share every time someone you personally connected to SHIFT buys AI credits.

03
ContentPay

Create on the platform and get paid when your shared content drives credit purchases. Not for sharing alone; it has to convert.

04
CreditPay

Podium payouts to top consumers, ranked on credits purchased and credits actually put to work.

05
CompetePay

A leaderboard across everything above, paid podium-style to the top performers.

Percentages, pool sizes and club thresholds are set by SHIFT and change over time. This page deliberately quotes none of them. The current numbers are shown inside the platform after you sign in.
